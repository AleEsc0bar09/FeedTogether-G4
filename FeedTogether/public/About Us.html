Meet FeedTogether

What is FeedTogether?

FeedTogether is a collaborative web platform designed to bridge the gap between solidarity and community need. We connect businesses, local establishments, and individual donors with shelters, soup kitchens, and volunteers. Our goal is to optimize the redistribution of food and essential supplies, transforming surplus into opportunities and tangible support for those who need it most.

Our Mission

Streamline and accelerate donation management through an accessible, interactive platform, ensuring that food and essential resources reach vulnerable communities and support centers in a dignified, rapid, and transparent manner.

Our Vision

To be the leading community network for management and social impact in the eradication of hunger at the local level, fostering a culture of zero waste, active collaboration, and sustainable development among citizens, organizations, and volunteers.
